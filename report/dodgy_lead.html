DodgysDD teaches one idea in several variations: after liquidity is swept, a fair value gap that price closes back through (an inversion FVG) signals the new direction, and he enters on that close. His videos and pages can't be reached from this research environment, so the rules come from his X thread and TradingView listings, his rating sheet, his indicator developer's docs and an open-source rebuild of his indicator. The canonical version below follows his own description; 71 other variants change the timeframe, grade filter, session, stop and exit. It ran on the same data and costs as everything else on this page, with no tuning.

It loses in both periods on both indices. Before costs the trades are a coin flip (about zero R per trade), and 1-minute stops of 2–5 points make costs a quarter of each trade. 282 of 288 variant runs lost money. Requiring his A+ sweep improves results slightly but doesn't make them positive.
